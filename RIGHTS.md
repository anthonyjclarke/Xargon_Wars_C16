# Rights and Preservation

## Historical Software

The surviving Xargon Wars source was written by A.J. Clarke "with kind permission from John B Marshall" and carries the notice `(C) MICRO-PROJECTS LTD. 1985`. The game shows "© GREMLIN GRAPHICS" on its title page. Those notices are all still in the preserved source and binaries. The game, its source and the other programs on the disk keep whatever rights status they already had.

The disk also holds third-party development tools: the "COMMODORE 64 EDITOR V072982 (C) 1982 BY COMMODORE BUSINESS MACHINES", "DOS V5.2 (C)BUGGA-SOFT 1984", a fast loader and an assembler. They're here because they're part of the disk image, and their rights stay with their owners.

---

## Printed Material and Artwork

The cassette inlay, instruction card, screenshot, logos and artwork stay with their rights holders. The three game images look like copies from an online archive (see [Artifacts](docs/ARTIFACTS.md)). I've included them as historical reference, and I don't know where they came from. The photo of my source disks is my own.

---

## Repository Status

I've set this repository up for studying, reassembling and preserving the historical material. I haven't assigned any new open-source or artwork licence. Making the repository public doesn't grant any rights beyond a licence that is expressly supplied.

The build scripts adapt the approach from my [Dorks Dilemma C16 repository](https://github.com/anthonyjclarke/Dorks_Dilemma_C16), where the recovery background and tool acknowledgements are kept. The build never rewrites the original input files.
